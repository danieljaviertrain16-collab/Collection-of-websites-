What is Soccer

Soccer is a sport played by 2 teams with a round ball that cannot be used with hands or carrying. The main purpose of this game is to kick the ball into the opponents net to score goals the most to try to win a game. Each team has 11 players and 1 goalkeeper to bring a total of 24 players on the field. The only execption of using hands in Soccer is the goalkeepers in the penatly area.

Soccer at College/High school areas

Soccer can be played at High School as competitive team sports at Secondary Schools managed by State athletic associations in each of their state. It can be played by teams at High School districts for Varsity team which is the highest level of competitive sport as part of school's athletic program. Most students who plays for varsity are either junior or seniors mostly. Students who are Freshman or Sophomore play for Jr Varsity who chose to play soccer in that grade.

Soccer in Professional Leagues

There are some Pro leagues that play Soccer around the world. They are meant for organized competitions in where club compete within different countries or any region. There are several leagues such as English Premier league, La Liga, Bundesliga and Major League Soccer. The English Premier league is a top-tier league based in England which is the highest level of the english football league. Major League soccer is a top professional league that is in United States and canada that featured 30 teams across the region.

Championships involving soccer

Each League has their own Playoff tournament to determine a champion. However there are international championships tournaments that can occur every 4 years. The most popular one is the FIFA world cup, that occurs every 4 years in which team countries quailifed play in group stages to determine the final 16 teams in the knockout stage. The next world cup will be in 2026 hosted by USA, Canada and Mexico. There is also Woman's world cup which is held a year after men's world cup.
