Former Slides

The original wild west water park in Wilderness opened in 2003. It consisted of having an originally play structure with 4 slides.

Originally Wild west had 4 body slides known as Bronc Buster. However 2 of the slides facing center were closed off and never was in use again. It also used to have a raft slide known as the wilderness express.

Both Attractions listed above were closed in 2022 as part of renovation that were replaced by newer slides.

Another Former Slide.

There were several slides that used to be located where the expanded parking lot is now.

The slides were closed around 2019 and were dismantled since then.
