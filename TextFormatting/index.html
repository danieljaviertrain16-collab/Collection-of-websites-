Angry Birds game

This game discusses Angry Birds Franchise. In this franchise, it involves a puzzle game being developed by rovio series about the birds having their eggs stolen by the green pigs as well as the culture itself of the franchise since 2009.

Gameplay

The game focuses on Enemies known as Green pigs, who stole the birds eggs and the main objective to use a slingshot to defeat the pigs and destroying structures built by them in order to retrive the eggs they stolen to overcome the challenges. If all the green pigs are cleared, the level is completed. However As levels go on, they can get more challenging as more obstacles could be added.

Red bird

Red Bird, referred to as red, is the main character of the franchise. He is a leader of the birds on piggy island. He is a desert cardinal type bird that covers most of his body, hence the name. Red has became an icon, which inspires fan art & memes on internet. He is known for possesses unique abilites that enchance the gameplay of the franchise.
